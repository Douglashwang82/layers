Bayou City Taiwanese Cultural Circle

A volunteer-run community group serving Houston's Taiwanese American families since 2011.

About us

Bayou City Taiwanese Cultural Circle (BCTCC) is an all-volunteer nonprofit based in Westchase, Houston. We host monthly potlucks, a Saturday Mandarin school, and an annual Lantern Festival walk at Levy Park. Membership is open to anyone interested in Taiwanese culture — you do not need to be Taiwanese to join.
